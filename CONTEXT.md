# CONTEXT.md — Domain Glossary for AI Document Workflow

## Entities & Terms

### Document
An official business transaction record (Invoice, Quotation, Purchase Order, Receipt) submitted to the system for intelligent extraction, validation, and multi-tier approval.

### Document Number
The canonical unique business identifier (e.g., `INV-2025-0891`, `QUO-2025-0312`) extracted from the face of the document or assigned by the counterparty.

### Line Item
An itemized product or service breakdown within a document containing a description, unit count (quantity), unit price, and extended sub-amount.

### Multi-Level Approval
A sequential, governance-enforced authorization process consisting of two mandatory tiers:
- **Level 1 (Operational Review)**: Conducted by a Department Manager to verify delivered goods/services, quotation scope, and operational requirement.
- **Level 2 (Financial Approval)**: Conducted by a Finance Controller / CFO to verify budget allocation, tax calculation validity, and fund release.

### Workflow Action
A state-advancing or corrective decision executed by an authorized persona on a document:
- **Approve**: Advances document from Level 1 to Level 2, or completes Level 2 to final Approved state.
- **Reject**: Halts processing permanently and marks document as Rejected with reasons.
- **Request Revision**: Sends the document back to the submitter with instructions for correction.

### AI Anomaly Flag
A warning generated by the Gemini AI inference engine when detected document data violates financial or business rules, such as mathematical discrepancies between stated tax rate and total tax amount, line item sum mismatches, or missing tax registration credentials.

### Audit Log
An immutable, chronological record of every system event, actor identity, action type, before-and-after values, and UTC timestamp, ensuring complete enterprise compliance and accountability.

### User Persona
A role-based access control (RBAC) profile:
- **Staff (Submitter)**: Uploads documents, initiates workflows, reviews own submissions.
- **Manager (Level 1 Approver)**: Authorizes operational validity and scope.
- **Finance (Level 2 Approver)**: Authorizes tax compliance and disbursement.
- **Auditor (Governance)**: Read-only access across all workflows, anomaly registers, and immutable audit ledgers.
